Privacy Policy

Arabic Script Practice — Last updated: July 2026

1. Who We Are

Arabic Script Practice (“the App”) is a handwriting practice tool available at writearabic.app and as a downloadable desktop application. The App is maintained as an open-source project at github.com/ExplorerZach/arabic-handwriting.

2. Data Collected

The App stores the following data exclusively on your device (in your browser's localStorage or the desktop app's local storage):

  • Practice data: handwriting scores, practice dates, spaced-repetition progress, XP, levels, and streak information.
  • Settings: theme preference, brush size, locale (English/Arabic), dark/light mode, daily goal, sound preferences, and high-contrast/reduced-motion toggles.
  • Study decks: user-created collections of letters, words, numbers, and diacritics for practice sessions.
  • API key: your optional OpenRouter API key (stored encrypted on desktop via Stronghold; plaintext in localStorage on web).

No account is required — you can use the App without providing any personal information. All data lives on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.

3. AI Feedback (OpenRouter)

When you tap “AI Feedback,” your handwriting drawing is sent to OpenRouter, a third-party AI provider, for analysis. The drawing image, the letter name, and a form description are transmitted to OpenRouter's servers. OpenRouter's privacy policy is available at openrouter.ai/privacy. According to OpenRouter, they do not store prompt data or use it for model training by default, but you should review their policy for the latest terms.

You control this: AI feedback is optional. You can use the App in offline/skip mode without an API key, and no drawing data is sent externally.

4. Hosting & Analytics (Vercel)

The web version is hosted on Vercel, which may collect standard server logs (IP address, user agent, request path) for operational purposes. We use Vercel Analytics (privacy-focused, no cookies, no personal data collection) to understand aggregate usage. Vercel's privacy policy is at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy. Vercel Analytics is disabled in the desktop app.

5. Data Retention

All practice data, settings, and history remain in your browser's localStorage until you clear your browser data or use the “Export/Import” tools in the Settings panel to manage it. The App itself never deletes your data unless you explicitly use the deletion feature. OpenRouter claims not to retain API prompt data.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access & portability: Use the “Export progress” button in Settings to download a complete JSON backup of all your data.
  • Deletion: You can clear individual settings or your entire browser data at any time. A “Delete All Data” option is available in Settings.
  • Withdraw consent: Remove your API key in Settings to stop AI feedback. Switch to skip/offline mode at any time.
  • Rectification: You can re-practice any letter to update your scores.

7. Desktop App (Tauri)

The desktop version runs locally on your computer. The API key is encrypted using the system keychain (Stronghold vault). No data is sent to us. The desktop app's WebView does not use cookies or service workers. All data storage is functionally identical to the web version.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

9. Cookies

The App does not use cookies. All data is stored in localStorage or (on desktop) the local filesystem. No tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or session cookies are set by the App.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or to report a concern:

  • Open an issue at github.com/ExplorerZach/arabic-handwriting/issues
  • Or contact the maintainer via GitHub.
